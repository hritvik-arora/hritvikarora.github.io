ABOUT

Quantifying what Rocks Record

I am a BS–MS student at the Indian Institute of Science Education and Research Kolkata, interested in computational approaches to understanding Earth materials and geological processes. My current interests lie at the intersection of geomechanics, deformation, and computational geoscience. I am particularly interested in developing numerical models and computational tools that turn complex geological processes into measurable, reproducible results.

I build tools that turn geological data into measurements that can be quantified, compared, and used to investigate how Earth materials deform and evolve.

The model uses Smoothed Particle Hydrodynamics (SPH) coupled with a thermal grid to simulate fluid, thermal and phase-change processes along a slipping fault.
